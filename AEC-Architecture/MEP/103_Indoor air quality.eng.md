People today spend about 90% of their day indoors. However, due to insufficient ventilation and concentrated indoor pollution sources, **indoor air is often more polluted than outdoor air**.

- **Sick House Syndrome** - Right after new construction or remodeling, formaldehyde and VOCs are emitted from wallpaper, flooring, adhesives, etc., causing irritation of the eyes/nose/throat and headaches. Before occupancy, **bake-out** (raise indoor temperature to 35-40 degC to release harmful substances, then ventilate; repeat 3-5 times) is effective.
- **Sick Building Syndrome** - In aging buildings, polluted ducts, poor ventilation, mold growth, and similar issues can cause headaches, fatigue, and reduced concentration. A common feature is that symptoms improve after leaving the building. Regular HVAC cleaning and ventilation are key.

> A common cause in both syndromes is **insufficient ventilation**. Ventilation is critical in daily life.

## 1. Indoor Pollutants

| Pollutant | Main Sources | Health Effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Particulate Matter PM10/PM2.5** | Cooking fumes, outdoor infiltration, tobacco smoke | Respiratory and cardiopulmonary disease. PM2.5 is classified by WHO as Group 1 carcinogen |
| **Carbon Dioxide (CO2)** | Occupant breathing, combustion appliances | Above 1,000 ppm: reduced concentration and headaches; indirect indicator of ventilation status |
| **Formaldehyde (HCHO)** | New furniture, wallpaper, adhesives, paint | Mucosal irritation; WHO Group 1 carcinogen. Emissions increase at higher temperature |
| **VOC (Volatile Organic Compounds)** | Paint, cleaning agents, fragrances, coating materials | Headaches, nausea; some compounds are carcinogenic |
| **Carbon Monoxide (CO)** | Incomplete combustion (gas range, boiler), fire | Colorless/odorless; life-threatening at high concentration. CO alarm installation is mandatory |
| **Radon (Rn)** | Soil and rock, concrete and granite | Group 1 carcinogen causing lung cancer; caution in basements/semi-basements |
| **Total Airborne Bacteria** | A/C filters, ducts, humidifiers, carpets | Respiratory infections and allergies; can increase rapidly in warm/humid environments |
| **Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2)** | Gas range, boiler, vehicle exhaust | Respiratory mucosal irritation; worsens asthma |
| **Ozone (O3)** | Copiers, printers, ion-type air cleaners | Eye/nose/throat irritation; reduced lung function |

## 2. Recommended Standards

The Indoor Air Quality Control Act manages pollutants in two categories:

- **Maintenance standards** - legally mandatory. Violations may incur an administrative fine up to KRW 10 million.
- **Recommended standards** - voluntary compliance. No direct fine for exceedance, but improvement measures may be requested in facilities used by vulnerable groups.

**Maintenance Standards**

| Pollutant | General Public-Use Facilities | Facilities Used by Vulnerable Groups ★ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PM10 | <= 100 ug/m3 | <= 75 ug/m3 |
| PM2.5 | <= 50 ug/m3 | <= 35 ug/m3 |
| CO2 | <= 1,000 ppm | <= 1,000 ppm |
| HCHO | <= 100 ug/m3 | <= 80 ug/m3 |
| Total Airborne Bacteria | <= 800 CFU/m3 | <= 500 CFU/m3 |
| CO | <= 10 ppm | <= 10 ppm |

> ★ Facilities for vulnerable groups: medical institutions, postpartum care centers, elderly care facilities, daycare centers, indoor children's play facilities.

**Recommended Standards**

| Pollutant | Recommended Standard |
| --- | --- |
| NO2 | <= 0.1 ppm |
| Rn | <= 148 Bq/m3 |
| TVOC | <= 500 ug/m3 |
| O3 | <= 0.06 ppm |

> Maintenance standards must be measured **once per year**, and recommended standards **once every 2 years**. Results must be retained for 10 years.

## 3. Air Quality Maintenance Standards by Facility Type and Size

**Indoor Air Quality Maintenance Standards by Facility Type** (Enforcement Rule of the Indoor Air Quality Control Act, Appendix Table 2, effective January 1, 2026)

| Facility Category | Applicable Facilities | PM10 (ug/m3) | PM2.5 (ug/m3) | CO2 (ppm) | Formaldehyde (ug/m3) | Total Airborne Bacteria (CFU/m3) | CO (ppm) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **General public-use facilities** | Subway stations, underground shopping malls, station waiting halls, passenger terminals, airports, funeral halls, movie theaters, academies, exhibition facilities, PC rooms, bathhouses | <= 100 | <= 50 | <= 1,000 | <= 100 | - | <= 10 |
| **Education/culture facilities** | Libraries, museums, art museums, large stores (department stores, hypermarkets), academies | <= 100 | **<= 40** | <= 1,000 | <= 100 | - | <= 10 |
| **Facilities used by vulnerable groups** ★ | Medical institutions, postpartum care centers, elderly care facilities, daycare centers, indoor children's play facilities | **<= 75** | **<= 35** | <= 1,000 | **<= 80** | **<= 500** | <= 10 |
| **Indoor parking lots** | Indoor parking facilities | <= 200 | - | <= 1,000 | <= 100 | - | **<= 25** |
| **Sports/performance/business facilities** | Indoor sports facilities, indoor performance halls, business facilities, mixed-use buildings | <= 200 | - | - | - | - | - |

> ★ Stricter criteria apply to vulnerable-group facilities for PM10, PM2.5, and formaldehyde.

> For libraries, movie theaters, academies, and PC rooms where natural ventilation is not possible and mechanical ventilation is used, the CO2 criterion must be maintained at **<= 1,500 ppm**.

**Mandatory Requirements by Size**

- **Total floor area >= 3,000 m2** or subway stations: mandatory ventilation system installation.
- **Total floor area >= 2,000 m2**: mandatory self-measurement of air quality and record retention.
- **New apartment complexes with >= 100 households**: mandatory indoor air quality measurement and public disclosure before occupancy.

**Penalties for Violations**

| Violation | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| Failure to meet maintenance standards | Administrative fine up to KRW 10 million + corrective order |
| Non-compliance with corrective order | Imprisonment up to 1 year or fine up to KRW 10 million |
| Failure to perform self-measurement / false submission | Administrative fine of KRW 2-5 million |
| Use of construction materials emitting pollutants | Administrative fine of KRW 5-20 million |
